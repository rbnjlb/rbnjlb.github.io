Grand Scheme Studio

The production tool of the YouTube channel The Grand Scheme.

The Grand Scheme tells the true stories of history's most audacious cons, heists, escapes and spy operations, with every fact sourced. Watch it on YouTube: youtube.com/@thegrandschemefiles.

What this application does

Grand Scheme Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the channel. Through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, it uploads and schedules the channel's own videos, sets their thumbnails, captions and descriptions, and reads the channel's own statistics to improve future videos. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.

Privacy policy

Grand Scheme Studio uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
